====================================================
CORE PRODUCT VISION
====================================================

MeetPulse is a next-generation AI collaboration platform that transforms traditional presentations, lectures, seminars, workshops, and business meetings into intelligent, interactive, and data-driven experiences.

MeetPulse is NOT a Zoom replacement.

Do NOT build video conferencing.

Instead, MeetPulse works alongside:

- Zoom
- Google Meet
- Microsoft Teams
- Physical classrooms
- Conferences
- Workshops

The meeting happens on any platform.

MeetPulse provides the intelligence layer:

- Interactive presentations
- Live audience engagement
- AI transcription
- AI summaries
- Smart attendance
- Audience understanding analysis
- Presenter coaching
- Analytics
- Meeting memory


The product should feel like a startup that combines:

Notion + Linear + Slack + Figma + Zoom + Otter.ai + Mentimeter

while maintaining its own unique visual identity.
